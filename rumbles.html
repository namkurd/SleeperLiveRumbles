QB Injury Backup Adjustments

  • Confirmed: the commissioner has added the backup's points to your official score.
  • Likely: your starter's status changed to Out during the game and a backup from his team came in and scored. The backup's points count toward your score. It becomes Confirmed once the commissioner enters it in Sleeper. Hover or tap the QB's name to see when the change was logged.
  • Possible: your QB played and a backup from his team also played, but his status was not caught changing to Out during the game. This could just be a normal substitution, so no points are added unless the commissioner confirms it.
  • Commissioner Adjustment is the number of points the commissioner actually added.

Actual

Official standings through the last completed week. During games, This Week and Pts This Week update live, and season totals update once the week is final. Use the week menu to look back at past weeks.

Projected

Where the standings would land if every player finishes at their projection.

Tap a team's points to see each player's score. Names share a color with this week's opponent.

The small EPA button (bottom left on a computer, at the very bottom on a phone) adds a total EPA column next to points: the combined expected points added by a team's starters, kickers and defenses included. Sort by it like any column, and hover or tap a number to see each player's EPA. Numbers appear after each game ends (updated daily), so a live week shows a faded partial total.

Rumblin'

Live cards for every team. Everyone shows all 12, Live only the managers with a player in a game, and Me your own view: Matchup, In Play (teams whose Rumble against you is still undecided), and Scenarios. You can also tap names in the strip to pick any managers to view.

Scenarios opens once the Sunday 1pm games end. It shows what you need to outscore in each Rumble still in play, and resets each game window.

Score Feed lists scoring plays as they happen (only while this page is open) and clears 4.5 hours after kickoff. Live Players shows who's in a game now. A football means his team has the ball, a picket fence a defense on the field. Faded cards have nobody in a game.

Your pick is remembered on this device. Copy link (in Me) opens straight to your view.
